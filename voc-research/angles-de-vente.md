## ANGLE 1 — "0.6 seconde" (la peur chiffrée)
**Verbatim source :** *"Standard jeans lasted about 0.6 seconds on a belt sander before giving up completely"* + *"Proper riding jeans... made it to 7 seconds"* (itsbetterontheroad).
**Idée FR :** Transformer "le jean normal protège un peu" (croyance n°1) en chiffre brutal et mémorisable. La seconde de glisse.
**Hooks EN :**
- "Your everyday jeans last **0.6 seconds** on tarmac. Ours are tested to CE AA."
- "Skin vs asphalt at 40mph. How long do your jeans buy you — half a second?"
- "0.6 seconds. That's how long denim lasts in a slide. Don't ride on borrowed time."
